A systems mindset, from silicon to signal

I'm a Communication and Electronics Engineering student at the Canadian International College, drawn to the point where hardware meets network meets code. Across coursework and six national training programs, I've worked through the full stack of a connected device: sensing and control on a microcontroller, the protocol that carries its data, the network that routes it, and the security review that checks it's safe to ship. I like problems that don't stay in one layer — a temperature sensor is only useful once its reading survives the trip from an ESP32 to a dashboard someone can actually act on.
